Privacy Notice

This website respects your privacy. It does not use cookies, does not include analytics or tracking services, does not embed third-party content, and does not offer contact forms. No personal data is collected, stored, or shared by this website itself.

Server log files

When you visit this website, the hosting provider may automatically process technical information — such as your IP address, the date and time of your request, the requested page, and your browser type — in server log files. This processing is technically necessary to deliver the website and to ensure its stability and security (legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, legitimate interest). Log files are not merged with other data sources and are routinely deleted by the hosting provider.

External links

This website contains links to external websites. This privacy notice does not apply to those sites; please refer to the privacy information of the respective operators.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, and objection. If you believe that the processing of your data violates data protection law, you may lodge a complaint with the Austrian Data Protection Authority (dsb.gv.at).
